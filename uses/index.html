Uses

Even though the state of tech is kind of depressing, I still love my gadgets and silly little apps.

Desktop

A so-called GAMING laptop that I managed to snatch with a discount. Compared to my previous desktops, I never had much trouble running the games I wanted to play, however the battery life is atrocious (Can't even last 2 hours) and becomes completely unusable and sluggish when disconnected from the wall.

  • Model: ASUS TUF Gaming F15 FX506HF
  • CPU: Intel Core i5-11400H
  • Memory: 16GB
  • Storage: 2TB
  • GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050 (+ Integrated Intel UHD Graphics)
  • Operating System: secureblue (Fedora 44)

Mobile

Bought specifically for use with GrapheneOS. It serves its purpose, but in the summer it frequently overheats, and the battery life is not anything to write home about either.

  • Model: Google Pixel 8
  • Chipset: Google Tensor G3
  • Memory: 8GB
  • Storage: 128GB
  • Operating System: GrapheneOS (Android 17)
